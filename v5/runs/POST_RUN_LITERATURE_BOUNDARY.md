# Literature boundary of the observed Phase 5 direction

Audit date: 2026-10-05. This is a **post-run reporting audit**, performed outside
the proposer and not fed into its research state. It does not modify the frozen
literature registry, verifier, recorded statuses or primary metrics.

**known:** Balan and Zou explicitly distinguish the natural phase-quotient
signal distance from the matrix norm distance of rank-one lifts. Section 2 of
their [primary paper](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1506.02092) gives expressions for
both the squared natural distance and squared Frobenius lift distance. The
paper also studies intensity stability in the lifted metric and the failure
of global equivalence near zero. These ideas are not new Phase 5 discoveries.

**proved-in-project:** an accepted Phase 5 SOS identity proves exactly its
typed polynomial inequality on its typed semialgebraic domain. The observed
trajectory supplies particular constants, error domains and algebraic
certificates within the existing lifting/stability framework. This is a
description of checked project work, not a priority claim.

**novelty-uncertain:** no exhaustive literature priority search was performed
for each selected constant or sufficient perturbation bound. An offline lookup
with no matching topic cannot establish originality. Physical meanings written
in proposal prose are not automatically within the formal verifier's scope.

The research claim supported by this pilot concerns proposal selection,
explicit failure feedback, certificate correction and separation of proposer
from acceptance authority. It must not be rewritten as discovery of phase
retrieval, a new complement-property theorem, or a complete general theory of
robust inverse problems.
