# Model and evidence contracts

Three explicitly different modes:

1. Complete finite prior: all worlds, targets and deterministic action responses
   are declared as the possible-world input. No actual world id is passed to
   the policy. Rational enclosing disks, hitting sets and minimax dynamic
   programming yield exact finite C*. Every world/reply is also checked against
   a continuous range contract, but the finite upper bound is not transferred
   to that larger contract. A simulator can execute the tree in each world.
2. Continuous hard-ball prior: observation-only candidate construction supplies
   a verified finite witness restriction. Its adaptive optimum is a physical
   cost lower bound because every offered action/reply is represented. Exact
   reference purchase plus arbitrary measurement subset yields a response-
   uniform physical upper bound. Search caps and missing UB remain unresolved.
3. Checked continuous exact reduction: an algebraic completeness certificate
   proves that an exact zero-noise tangency prior contains precisely two
   reflection worlds. Only after this check may both finite bounds transfer
   to the continuous problem. Three scales exercise this narrow proof; they
   are not three independent discoveries or a general reduction method.

World ids in the finite catalog are hypotheses, not truth labels. Complete
catalog membership is a strong external modeling assumption. Targets are
legitimate prior data only in that explicit finite mode. The continuous agent
receives observations and public physical contracts, never private simulator
targets or future realized measurements. Potential responses in a checked
witness world are derived from that hypothetical world, not the actual one.

Only existing service kinds are used: exact absolute reference, ordinary range
and trusted intrinsic baseline. The uniform UB family uses references only;
the continuous LB must include the full offered menu. Future ordinary replies
chosen for witness restrictions satisfy the GLOBAL clean-noise budget together
with original ranges. They do not claim to cover all adversarial future replies.

q counts measurement coordinates, r whole source fault groups. Positive subset
covers retain all source-deletion branches. Uniform prospective UB currently
supports hard-ball priors without soft-source reports; actual subset recovery
supports the frozen grouped-source union. No physical root-validation protocol.

Reported primary metrics: lower/upper cost, absolute/relative optimality gap,
finite exact optima, unresolved instances, automatic old-certificate repairs,
subset nodes, uniform certificate nodes, witness counts, adaptive states and
runtime. Search completeness refers only to the stated finite enumeration;
nonlinear candidate search remains incomplete. A certificate-family failure
does not establish physical impossibility. Null upper means unknown, while
proved infinite lower is separately flagged.

Source/config/seed/evaluator freeze before held-out. All prior files are
immutable. Runtime measurements are separate from deterministic scientific
artifacts so reproduction can compare scientific bytes. One fixed regression,
six finite template families, five general continuous template families and
three scales of one analytic continuous reduction are declared in config.json.
These are small synthetic problems, not a broad benchmark.
