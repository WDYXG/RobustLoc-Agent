publication. All pre-v3 tracked files are frozen, including v1, all v2 sources,
reports and runs. V1_V2_FREEZE.json checks those bytes before/after runs. Do not
edit old README/AGENTS or old experiments. Reuse immutable v2 mathematics only.

refuted. No originality, autonomous theorem discovery or general nonlinear
inverse-problem capability claim. This is a finite decision/planning agent.

measurements belong to simulator/evaluator. Certificates require a declared
conditional q budget, fixed calibrated weights, valid domain and clean residual
budget. Estimated q and learned probabilities cannot authorize recovery.
Zero lower bound means not certified; only an upper witness proves insufficiency.

Freeze new sources/config/seeds before final evaluation. All methods use identical
episodes/observation offers. Log every action, certification failure, exhausted
budget and out-of-contract stress case. Report risk, coverage and sensing cost
together. Compare active to passive, random and immediate-gain greedy policies.
