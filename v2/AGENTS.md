# Theory-driven v2 contract

Never mutate ../runs/run-001, historical v1 methods, metric results or reports.
Check V1_FREEZE.json before and after each v2 execution. No CEP90 incumbent
selection. This scope is static exact-anchor ranges with at most q arbitrary
distance corruptions; uncertain anchors require a separate extension.

First audit primary literature. Lower frame bounds, FIM/GDOP, 2q sparse
observability, trimming and residual weighting are known foundations. Every
claim has an ID, one of the five evidence labels, assumptions, provenance,
proof/witness pointers and a novelty field. proved-in-project means a written
deduction checked in this project, not new to the literature or formally verified.

One conjecture at a time: derive/prove or construct a counterexample; implement
an independent numerical check; preserve a hash-linked append-only record;
reformulate only with explicit assumptions. Do not count pytest as proof.
Local certificates do not imply global branch identification. Positive frame
margin does not imply L1/IRLS or an arbitrary solver recovers corrupted data.
Residual-based geometry weights are not Fisher information without a likelihood.

Fixed v2 experiments stress assumptions, ambiguity, bound ratios and abstention.
No tuning on v1 cases. Verifier recomputes certificates independently. No public
novelty claim until further audit; new estimator is a project proposal only.
